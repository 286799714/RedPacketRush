# Traditional playing-card faces

The 52 PNG card faces in this directory come from **Bridge-Sized Playing Card
Deck (PNG, CC0)** by Mesmedir:

- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/bridge-sized-playing-card-deck-png-cc0
- Original download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/playingcards_bridgesize_png.zip
- License: Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0)
- License deed: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The source page describes the pack as a derivative of Dmitry Fomin's 2017
Wikimedia Commons collection. The files are redistributed here unchanged; only
the two card backs and two jokers from the archive were omitted because the game
does not display them.
